Our services

Every one ends the same way: a dog that feels refreshed and smells amazing.

Luxurious Bath & Dry

  1. 01

    Luxurious Bath & Dry

    A bath with a dry brush, nail trimming, and grooming of the face, paws and sanitary area. We use specially selected shampoos tailored for your dog.

    from $40

  2. 02

    Mini Groom

    Perfect for puppies or a tidy up between full grooms.

    Bath, complete drying, brushing, nail trimming, and grooming of the face, paws and sanitary area. Keeps your dog clean and fresh between full grooming appointments.

    from $60

  3. 03

    Full Groom

    Fully styled haircut perfectly suited to your needs.

    A bath, thorough drying, brushing, nail trimming, and a stylish clip for the face, feet and sanitary areas. Long coat or short coat, we make sure your dog looks and feels fantastic.

    from $90

  4. 04

    Standard Groom

    A complete all-over clip including body, legs and face at one length, with no scissor work on the face (depending on fur condition). Thorough wash, gentle blow dry, nails, brushing, paw pads and sanitary area.

    Poodle or spaniel and want scissor work on certain areas? That is a Full Groom.

  5. 05

    De-Shedding

    Full wash with a de-shedding shampoo and fur-release conditioner, using a de-shedding bath attachment, then a blow dry and brush with de-shedding tools. Natural look with a tidy finish, or scissor shaping.

    Scissor shaping is $10 per body part. A full body outline trim is a flat $30, any size.

    from $85

  6. 06

    Fancy Breed Groom

    A tailored groom for breeds that need it, including x-breeds like Oodles. Pick a show or creative groom that suits your dog. The skill and time vary with the style you choose.

    from $105

  7. 07

    Colour Dye

    Add a dye to any service when you book, or book a dyeing session on its own. A pampering that reflects their personality.

    Ask when you book.
